Your interactive guide to RF engineering and amateur radio

RF-Hub is an interactive RF engineering education platform for aspiring and licensed amateur radio operators. Whether you're sitting your Foundation licence or digging into antenna impedance matching, you'll find structured reference material, hands-on interactive tools, and field experiment write-ups here.

The site covers the full chain from electromagnetic theory through to practical station operation — frequency allocations, antenna design, propagation, and measurement technique. Everything is free, no account required to read.

How to use RF-Hub

Step 01

Browse by topic

Use the navigation above to jump to Spectrum, Antennas, Sidebands tools, or the Blog for in-depth articles.

Step 02

Follow the curriculum

The Learn path takes you from EM radiation basics through to antenna measurement in 26 structured modules.

Step 03

Use interactive tools

Calculators, 3D visualisations, and live WSPR data give you a feel for how RF behaves in the real world.
