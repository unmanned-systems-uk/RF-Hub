Learning Moments
  • Select DC V and place probes across any two nodes to read voltage.
  • Select DC A with RED on SER tap and BLACK on GND to read current in series.
  • Ammeter across voltage source (DC A, VP to GND) → short-circuit warning.
  • Ω on a live circuit → protection warning; always power off before measuring resistance.
  • Diode test: forward-biased LED reads Vf ≈ 2.1 V; reversed gives OL.
  • DMM input impedance ≈ 10 MΩ — negligible loading on this circuit.
