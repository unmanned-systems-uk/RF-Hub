Model Notes
MUF = foF2 × sec(θ) = foF2 × √(1 + (d / 2h_F2)²)
foF2 varies diurnally: peaks ~14:00 local (F2 layer electron density max)
SFI ∝ solar UV/X-ray flux → drives ionisation; higher SFI → higher foF2 → higher MUF
LUF: D-layer absorption sets lower limit; D layer exists only in daylight, dissolves after sunset
Night: LUF drops to ~1–2 MHz; MUF reduced but F2 layer persists

This is a simplified mid-latitude model for educational illustration.
Real propagation: VOACAP, DX Toolbox, or the Ofcom propagation model.
