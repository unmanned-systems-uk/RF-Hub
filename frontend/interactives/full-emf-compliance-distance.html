Formula
d = √( P × (DC/100) × G / (4π × Sref) )
G = 10^(dBi / 10)  ·  linear gain

Sref per ICNIRP 1998 Table 8 — general public:
  f < 1 MHz → 20.08 W/m²
  1–10 MHz → 20.08/f W/m²
  10–400 MHz → 2 W/m²
  400–2000 MHz → f/200 W/m²
  > 2 GHz → 10 W/m²

Sources: ICNIRP 1998 guidelines; Ofcom EMF Calculator guidance note (2021). Sref values are power density reference levels for general public (not occupational). This tool is for educational illustration — always consult the official Ofcom EMF self-assessment tool and your licence conditions.
