## Notes

- Page numbers refer to pages in the RSGB Intermediate Licence manual

- Sub-section letters (e.g. "2C") group related outcomes
- Some refs appear on multiple pages (topic spans multiple chapters)
- Not all sub-sections from the exam appear here (e.g. no 1A1 — may be Foundation-only)
- Refs like 3B, 3F, 3L, 4H, 6F, 7F, 9D have content but aren't in our exam question bank yet
