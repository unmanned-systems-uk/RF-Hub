## 1. Current books (verified on rsgbshop.org, 2026-09-27)

**Note on the shop domain:** the task brief named `shop.rsgb.org`, but that hostname doesn't resolve (DNS failure, checked directly). The RSGB's actual live shop is **`www.rsgbshop.org`** — linked from rsgb.org's own publications pages. A second domain, `rsgbstore.org.uk`, also turns up in search results but displays a banner stating it's "a backup site for www.rsgbshop.org... currently suspended" and only serves stale 2019-edition listings — **do not use rsgbstore.org.uk links**, they're superseded.

All three manuals are currently in their **3rd Edition**, covering RSGB syllabus V1.6 (in force for exams from 1 September 2024 onward). No 4th edition exists for any level as of the check date — searches for a 2025/2026 successor edition turned up nothing beyond a 2025 reprint of the Foundation book (same 3rd-edition content, ISBN unchanged).

Plain, dyslexia-friendly, no endorsement/affiliation implied:

> RF-Hub's study guides complement the RSGB licence manuals — they don't replace them. We recommend studying alongside the official RSGB book for your licence level.
