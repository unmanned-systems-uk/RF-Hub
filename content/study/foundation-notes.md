## Audio Frequency Range (Section 2 or 3)

**Exam question type:** "What is the acceptable audio frequency range for
communications?" / "What frequencies should an audio amplifier pass?"

**Answer:** 300 Hz to 3,000 Hz (3 kHz)

- This is **communications-grade audio**, not hi-fi (which is 20 Hz to 20 kHz)
- An audio amplifier in a transceiver is expected to pass 300 Hz – 3 kHz
  and filter out frequencies outside this range
- The 300 Hz lower limit removes low-frequency hum and rumble
- The 3 kHz upper limit removes high-frequency hiss and keeps bandwidth narrow
- This bandwidth is sufficient for intelligible speech

**Why it catches people:** Most people think of "audio" as 20 Hz – 20 kHz
(the human hearing range). The exam is asking about the communications audio
range, which is much narrower.

**Memory aid:** "300 to 3k — just enough to talk"

Source: Anthony's Foundation exam experience — question appeared but not
well covered in standard training materials.
