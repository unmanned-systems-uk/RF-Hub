### The UK licence progression (2 minutes)

Briefly and warmly explain the path for anyone unfamiliar:

- **Foundation** — the entry-level licence. 25-question exam, 25 W PEP power limit, callsign format M3/M6/M7. Absolutely achievable in a few weeks of study — this is the recommended starting point for most people.
- **Intermediate** — deeper theory. 45-question exam, 100 W PEP, callsigns 2E0/2E1. Homebrew construction rights.
- **Full** — the full licence. 62 questions (post-2024), 105 minutes, 74% pass mark, 1000 W PEP on most HF bands, callsigns M0/M1/M5. Also grants CEPT reciprocal operating in ~50 countries.
- **Direct-to-Full (D2F)** — a fast-track path for Foundation holders skipping Intermediate. Same 62-question exam. Requires the same depth of theory as taking Intermediate + Full separately, but done in one go.

Mention the **post-2024 Ofcom licence review** briefly — power limits were increased in September 2024 (Foundation went 10→25 W, Intermediate 50→100 W, Full 400→1000 W). Older study material may still quote the old numbers — RF-Hub content is aligned with the current framework.

## Regulatory accuracy — DO NOT deviate

- **Post-2024 Ofcom powers:** Foundation **25 W**, Intermediate **100 W**, Full **1000 W (1 kW)** on most HF bands.
- **60m Full:** 100 W PEP with a 200 W EIRP cap.
- **Full exam post-2024:** 62 questions, 105 min, 74% pass, two-part format.
- **CEPT T/R 61-01** = Full licence (HAREC) only. Do not describe it as Foundation/Intermediate.
- **Encryption prohibited** under UK licence terms.
- **Foundation audio range:** 300 Hz – 3 kHz (communications-grade).
- **EMF compliance** is a **legal requirement** for all UK licensees since 2021.

If uncertain, phrase carefully or omit — do not guess.
