## Notes

- **Chapter timestamps are estimates** for a 20-minute target. Once the audio is generated and you have the actual runtime, update the timestamps to match (YouTube auto-generates chapters from the description if you follow the `0:00 Title` format).
- **First chapter MUST start at 0:00** for YouTube auto-chapters to work.
- **Update the D2F line** ("Or already licensed and…") when the D2F curriculum goes live on the site.
- **First 150 characters** are what shows in YouTube search snippets and shorts previews — make sure the opening sentence is compelling.
- **Hashtag cap:** YouTube uses up to **3 hashtags** as clickable tags above the title (the first 3 in the description). Rest are for keyword coverage.
- **Custom thumbnail** matters as much as title for click-through — separate design task, not included here.
