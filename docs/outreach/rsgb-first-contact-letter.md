Dear Sir or Madam,

**RF-Hub: a free, accessible learning resource for Intermediate and Full licence candidates**

I am writing to introduce RF-Hub (rf-hub.info), a free online study resource I have been building over the past year, and to ask whether the RSGB would be willing to take a brief look at it and share any feedback.

I should start with a brief explanation of why it exists.

I am a self-taught amateur radio enthusiast currently working towards my Full licence, having passed my Foundation assessment some time ago. I am based in the Scottish Borders (grid IO85LO) and hold the SWL callsign G/SWL/EH43. I am dyslexic, and I found the traditional text-heavy preparation materials — while comprehensive and authoritative — difficult to work through at pace. I learn much more effectively from structured visual content, interactive diagrams, and worked examples presented alongside the theory rather than separately.

RF-Hub is the resource I wished had existed when I started studying. It covers the Intermediate and Full Licence syllabuses with visual explanations, interactive tools, and structured self-check questions designed to work well for learners who find dense technical text a barrier. The site currently includes:

- Structured study sections for the Intermediate syllabus (Sections 1–4 complete, further sections in progress)
- An interactive antenna curriculum of twenty lessons covering everything from fundamental EM radiation through to phased arrays
- Reference sidebands on topics such as S-parameters, VSWR measurement, digital modes, and propagation
- An RSA5065N spectrum analyser measurement laboratory
- [X unique visitors this month | Y chapters read | Z self-check questions answered]

I want to be clear about what RF-Hub is and is not. It is a companion resource and revision aid — not a replacement for the RSGB Intermediate Licence Manual or any other RSGB publication. The RSGB manuals are, in my view, the authoritative written texts for amateur licence preparation, and the site acknowledges this explicitly. RF-Hub exists to help learners engage with the material from the manuals in a more interactive way; it regularly directs learners back to the relevant RSGB source chapters.

The site carries an attribution notice and a clear disclaimer that it is an independent resource, not affiliated with or endorsed by the RSGB.

My ask is a modest one. I am not seeking endorsement, content licensing, or any kind of formal partnership. I would simply like to know whether someone at the RSGB would be willing to look at the site and let me know if there is anything they would want changed before it becomes something the RSGB would be comfortable pointing learners to as a supplementary study aid.

I am entirely open to making any adjustments the RSGB considers appropriate — strengthening attribution, removing any content that raises concerns, or adjusting how the relationship between RF-Hub and the official materials is presented. I am also willing to promote RSGB publications more prominently if that would be welcome.

I recognise the RSGB has a considerable workload in supporting the UK amateur community, and I am grateful for any time you are able to give this.

If you would like to look at the site, it is at rf-hub.info. I am happy to provide any further information or to discuss the site at a time convenient to you.
